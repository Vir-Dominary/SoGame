# SoGame Trademark and Brand Usage Policy

**Status: This document is a brand usage policy. It is NOT a trademark
registration certificate.**

SoGame has **not** registered "SoGame" as a trademark (as of the date of
this document). Nothing in this document should be read as a claim that
"SoGame" is a registered trademark, and this document does not create or
evidence any trademark registration. The "®" symbol is not used because
no registration exists.

## 1. Software License vs. Brand

The SoGame software is distributed under the GNU Affero General Public
License version 3 (AGPLv3). The AGPLv3 grants rights in the **software
code** (copying, modification, redistribution, and — subject to the
AGPLv3 conditions — sale). The AGPLv3 does **not** grant any rights to
use the "SoGame" name, logos, icons, or any other brand identifiers.

SoGame is the name of the upstream project maintained by the SoGame
project maintainers. The name and logos of SoGame are separate assets
from the code and are not licensed under AGPLv3.

## 2. What Third-Party Distributions May Do

Anyone redistributing SoGame code (for example, a fork or a repackaged
build) may refer to SoGame for **factual attribution**, such as:

- "Based on SoGame"
- "A fork of SoGame"
- "Built from the SoGame source code"
- "Uses SoGame networking components"

Third-party forks may use their own project name and branding.

Such factual references must be truthful and must not imply endorsement,
sponsorship, or affiliation with the upstream SoGame project.

## 3. What Is Not Allowed Without Explicit Authorization

Unless you have explicit written authorization from the SoGame project
maintainers, you may not use the SoGame name or branding in a way that
would mislead users into believing that your distribution is:

- an official SoGame release;
- an official SoGame server, client, or service;
- an official SoGame installer or support channel;
- an officially licensed or authorized version of SoGame;
- endorsed, sponsored, or otherwise affiliated with the SoGame project;
- the official upstream SoGame project itself.

Names or phrases that suggest official association (such as "Official
SoGame", "SoGame Official", "SoGame Cloud", "SoGame Pro", or similar)
may not be used for third-party distributions without authorization.

This includes, but is not limited to: using the SoGame name or logo as
your own product name or logo, registering domain names that include
"SoGame" in a way that suggests official status, and using SoGame
branding in promotional materials without authorization.

## 4. No Modification of License Terms

This policy does not restrict the rights granted by the AGPLv3. In
particular, it does not prohibit forking, modification, redistribution,
or sale of the SoGame software code under the AGPLv3 terms. The purpose
of this policy is limited to protecting users from confusion about the
origin and official status of distributions, and to reserving the
project's rights in its name and branding for the future.

## 5. Future Trademark Registration

SoGame may apply for trademark registration in the future. If and when
a trademark is registered, this policy may be revised to reflect the
legal protections available under applicable trademark law. Until then,
this document is a voluntary brand usage policy based on the project's
rights in its name and goodwill, and on applicable unfair-competition
and consumer-protection laws where they apply.

## 6. Three Distinct Concepts

To avoid confusion, please keep these three concepts separate:

1. **Software license** — AGPLv3 applies to the SoGame original source
   code. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).
2. **Copyright** — Copyright in the SoGame code belongs to the SoGame
   contributors. See [NOTICE](NOTICE).
3. **Trademark / brand** — The "SoGame" name and logos are governed by
   this policy, not by the AGPLv3. See this document.
